<div class="space-y-6 mb-8">

**Black Ops Royale (BOR) is the no-loadout, ground-loot battle royale in the Black Ops 7 Season&nbsp;4 rotation** — a Blackout-inspired (Black Ops 4 lineage) mode on the **Avalon** map. 100 players, **Quads (4-max squads)**, developed by Treyarch / Raven under Activision. It's a Warzone mode, not a separate purchase.

This is a complete PC / M&K crew guide for a free-to-play group of 5–8 splitting across quads. The core mental shift: **loot decisions replace buy decisions.** You aren't building a gun — you're finding one, committing to it, and climbing its rarity. Snapshot as of **July&nbsp;3, 2026** and subject to playlist rotation.

</div>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Status Check — Is It Even Live?">
**Yes — BOR is live in the July 2026 rotation, as a Quads-only playlist.** Solos was removed at Season&nbsp;4 launch. The mode cycles, so **confirm it's in the playlist before a crew night**; if it's rotated out, the fallback is standard BR on Avalon / Verdansk. *(Community-tracked / playlist-dependent — verify in-client.)*
</Callout>

## <Crosshair className="inline-block mr-2 mb-1" size={28} /> Weapons — You're NOT Prioritizing the Same Guns

<Callout variant="info" title="Archetype Over Gun Name">
Guns are role-locked by **Build Archetype** (CQB, Raider, Covert, Viper, Heavy Metal, Stalker, Recon, etc.). The archetype — not the raw gun name — tells you the job: close-range pressure, mid-range beam, long-range, stealth, or mobility.
</Callout>

## <Sparkles className="inline-block mr-2 mb-1" size={28} /> Attachment Kits & Rarity Strategy

<div class="space-y-6 mb-8">

Attachment Kits (**purple glow**, easy to spot) are the real power curve. Each kit raises the **currently held** weapon one rarity tier and unlocks its next fixed attachment.

</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 mb-8">
  <div class="p-5 bg-card/50 rounded-lg border-l-4 border-primary">
    <div class="text-sm font-semibold text-primary mb-2">1 · Hold, Then Grab</div>
    <p class="text-xs text-foreground/80">Hold the weapon you're committing to <strong>before</strong> grabbing the kit. Kits upgrade the gun in your hands — never waste one on a throwaway.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-5 bg-card/50 rounded-lg border-l-4 border-primary">
    <div class="text-sm font-semibold text-primary mb-2">2 · Commit Early, Feed It</div>
    <p class="text-xs text-foreground/80">Swapping guns resets your rarity investment. A Legendary gun that fits your role beats a "better" gun stuck at Common.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-5 bg-card/50 rounded-lg border-l-4 border-primary">
    <div class="text-sm font-semibold text-primary mb-2">3 · Archetype First, Rarity Second</div>
    <p class="text-xs text-foreground/80">Match the archetype to your squad role first, then climb rarity. Chase the role you need filled, not a gun name.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-5 bg-card/50 rounded-lg border-l-4 border-primary">
    <div class="text-sm font-semibold text-primary mb-2">4 · Check the Upgrade Path</div>
    <p class="text-xs text-foreground/80">Use the <strong>Upgrade Path</strong> tab on the Tac-Map to see what your held weapon still needs.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## <RotateCcw className="inline-block mr-2 mb-1" size={28} /> Redeploy & Survival — No Gulag

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8">

    <div class="text-sm font-semibold text-primary mb-2">🎟️ Redeploy Token</div>

  </div>
  <div class="p-6 bg-card/50 rounded-lg border border-border">
    <div class="text-sm font-semibold text-primary mb-2">🗼 Redeployment Tower</div>
    <p class="text-xs text-foreground/80">Teammate-activated; strong but a predictable pressure magnet. Don't capture blind — check high ground, use smoke / Sensor Dart, assign a cover man.</p>
  </div>

<Callout variant="danger" title="Death Is More Punishing Than Standard BR">
A careless death with **no token in the bank** is a hard out until a teammate can tower-revive. Always bank a Redeploy Token before pushing.
</Callout>

## <Package className="inline-block mr-2 mb-1" size={28} /> Perks & Equipment

<div class="space-y-6 mb-8">

Perks are **temporary lootable consumables** (~2–4 min each), hold up to 5, **manually activated**, and shareable with teammates. Bank them and pop for a specific push / revive / rotation / late-zone fight rather than treating them as always-on.

</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8">

## <Move className="inline-block mr-2 mb-1" size={28} /> Movement & M&K

<div class="space-y-6 mb-8">

Movement uses the BO7 system — wingsuit insertion, Grappling Hook, and omnimovement on the ground. Aim assist / input balance mirrors standard Warzone: **no input-based matchmaking separates you from controllers.** Plan around it, don't expect protection from it.

</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8">
  <div class="p-6 bg-primary/10 rounded-lg border-2 border-primary/40">
    <div class="text-xs text-green-400 font-semibold mb-2">WHERE YOU WIN</div>
    <p class="text-sm text-foreground/80"><strong>Range, through tracking and recoil control.</strong> Play power positions and pre-aim lanes.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-6 bg-card/30 rounded-lg border border-red-500/30">
    <div class="text-xs text-red-400 font-semibold mb-2">WHERE YOU LOSE</div>
    <p class="text-sm text-foreground/80"><strong>Chaotic CQB</strong>, where controller rotational aim assist is strongest. Use cover, angles, and utility to reset — don't take unnecessary close brawls.</p>
  </div>
</div>
