<div class="space-y-6 mb-8">

**This is the standard-BR loadout meta for Warzone, Black Ops 7 Season&nbsp;4** — the guns you build in Gunsmith and call in. It's tuned for a free-to-play mouse & keyboard crew, prioritizing recoil control and range where you win without aim assist.

Weapon tiers below are community-tested TTK (WZStats / TrueGameData-style), **not Activision-confirmed** — the meta is volatile, so treat this as a **July&nbsp;3, 2026** snapshot and re-verify each patch. For the *no-loadout* ground-loot meta, see the [Black Ops Royale guide](/archive/call-of-duty/black-ops-royale) instead.

</div>

<Callout variant="success" title="Free-to-Play Reality — Read First">
**Every meta weapon below is unlockable and levelable without owning BO7.** Grind weapon XP in-mode (**Plunder** is the efficient path) and unlock attachments normally. Battle-pass and store blueprints are convenience/cosmetic — *not* a power gate. You are **not** locked out of the current meta as a free player.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="danger" title="PC Anti-Cheat Gate">
Season&nbsp;4 requires **TPM&nbsp;2.0 + Secure Boot** for Microsoft Azure Attestation (MAA). One party member failing attestation pushes the whole squad to Casual matchmaking — verify every crew PC. Full setup in the [Settings & Mechanics guide](/archive/call-of-duty/settings).
</Callout>
